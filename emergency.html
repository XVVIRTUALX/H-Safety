PutaiaoTech Health & Safety – Emergency Procedures

Immediate Emergency Response

  • Follow site evacuation and assembly procedures.
  • Call emergency services if required (111).
  • Notify supervisor or Health & Safety officer immediately.
  • Do not place yourself or others at further risk.

Fire Emergency

  • Activate nearest fire alarm.
  • Use fire extinguishers only if trained.
  • Proceed to designated assembly point.
  • Await further instructions.

Medical Emergency

  • Contact emergency services immediately.
  • Administer first aid if trained.
  • Keep casualty stable until help arrives.
