PutaiaoTech Health & Safety

Construction Site Audit

Site hazards identified, documented and communicated to workers.

Fall protection systems, harnesses and guardrails in place.

Scaffolding erected and inspected by competent persons.

Portable tools tested and tagged, temporary power installed safely.

Excavators, cranes and other machinery inspected and operated by trained staff.

Workers using PPE including helmets, boots, gloves and high-visibility clothing.

Subcontractors briefed on site safety procedures and hazards.

Emergency response plans, first aid kits and incident reporting procedures available.
